# Captions without an NLE

**Job:** burned-in / karaoke captions without opening Premiere.

Use the hosted editor at [vidmoat.com](https://vidmoat.com), or send the clip to [@VidmoatBot](https://t.me/VidmoatBot).

```
Transcribe with word-level timings.

Burn karaoke captions:
- 4 to 6 words on screen at a time
- highlight the spoken word
- do not cover faces

Preview frames at 0:04, 0:12, and 0:20 as images.
If a caption sits on a face, move it. Then render.
```

For the brand pass (font, fill, stroke, lower-third), copy [karaoke-captions](./karaoke-captions.md). Do not restyle from scratch here.
