# Karaoke captions

**Job:** word-level captions that survive a muted scroll.

```
Transcribe with word-level timings.

Add karaoke captions:
- 4 to 6 words on screen at a time
- highlight the spoken word
- high contrast, bottom-safe for TikTok UI
- do not cover faces or products

Preview frames at 0:04, 0:12, and 0:20 as images.
If a caption sits on a face, move it. Then render.
```

Brand pass:

```
Restyle every caption to font Inter, weight 700, fill #FFFFFF, stroke 4px #000000, position lower-third. Keep word timings. Preview three frames as images before render.
```
