# Silence and filler

**Job:** cut dead air and ums without killing comic timing.

```
Transcribe this project with word-level timings.

1. Remove silences longer than 0.45s, except pauses after a joke, a question, or a name.
2. Remove filler: um, uh, you know, like (when like is a tick, not a comparison).
3. Close the gaps. Do not change the order of sentences.
4. Preview frames at three random cuts as images, plus 2 seconds of audio context if you can describe it.
5. List every cut you made (start, end, reason). I will restore any that ruin a beat.
6. Do not render until I say so.
```

If you are editing a comedy or a story:

```
Be conservative. Prefer leaving a 0.3s breath to cutting on the laugh. Show me the candidate cuts before applying them.
```

Vidmoat keeps these as editable timeline cuts, not a flattened MP4. That is the point versus a black-box clipper.
